A straight shot up I-15, done carefully

Escondido sits right on I-15, the same freeway that runs all the way to Las Vegas. It is a straightforward route on paper, but it climbs through the Cajon Pass and up to Mountain Pass before dropping into the Las Vegas valley, with serious summer heat in between.

Jose tows the whole way himself, sends updates from the road, and delivers to your home, storage lot or RV park in southern Nevada.
