How the quick estimate works

In town, RV moves are priced by the hour. The hourly rate depends on the length of your RV, and the clock runs from hookup to unhook, so the state of the pickup and drop-off spots matters. Cars, classics, golf carts, motorcycles and boats have a $150 local minimum.

You get two numbers. The first is the fair price if everything goes smoothly. The second covers extra time. Long-distance moves are priced per mile on the exact locations, the condition of the vehicle and the weather, so for those you send your details and Jose quotes it himself.

Important

This estimate can change when you talk with Jose. He can adjust it in your favor, or depending on your conditions: access at pickup and drop-off, the condition of the vehicle and the exact length of your RV or trailer. A minimum rate applies in San Diego.

Whatever the final number is, it will be fair. That's Jose's guarantee.

Quick estimate questions

Is the online estimate my final price?

No. It is an approximate range based on what you enter. The estimate can change when you talk with Jose: he can adjust it in your favor, or depending on your conditions, like access at pickup and drop-off, the condition of your vehicle and the exact length of your RV or trailer. Your final quote will always be fair. Guaranteed.

How are in-town RV moves priced?

In San Diego County and the Temecula area, RV moves are priced by the hour, and the hourly rate depends on the length of the RV. The clock runs from the moment Jose hooks up to the moment he unhooks, so a tight spot or a slow hookup adds time. A minimum rate applies.

Why are there two numbers?

The first number is the fair price if everything goes smoothly. The second one covers the extra time that sometimes shows up at pickup or drop-off, or in traffic.

Why doesn't it show a price for long distance?

Long-distance moves, like San Diego to Arizona, Las Vegas or Northern California, are priced per mile based on the exact locations, the condition of your vehicle and the weather. Send your details to Jose and he will reply with a fair quote.

How much is a local car tow?

In town, cars, classics, golf carts, motorcycles and boats have a $150 minimum. Longer in-town moves cost a bit more, and the estimate shows you the range.

Why do you need my name and phone number?

So Jose knows who the estimate is for and can reply with your quote. Your details only go to Jose, by your own text or email.

Tell Jose what needs to move

Call or text with the pickup, the drop-off and what you are hauling. You will get an honest answer and a fair quote, guaranteed.
