How the quick estimate works

The range is built the same way Jose prices a move: the miles from his base in Escondido to your pickup, the loaded miles to your drop-off, part of the drive home, diesel for the RAM 3500, driver time for driving and hookup, the tow fee for your kind of vehicle, and extras like a backyard pickup, a steep driveway or a vehicle that doesn't run.

Long trips include an overnight on the road. Same-day requests include a rush charge. Everything is shown line by line so you can see where the number comes from.

Important

This is an approximate estimate, not a quote. Jose has to review your information, and the final quote can change depending on the vehicle, its condition, access at pickup and drop-off, the route and timing.

Whatever the final number is, it will be fair. That's Jose's guarantee.

Quick estimate questions

Is the online estimate my final price?

No. It is an approximate range based on the details you enter and the driving route. Jose reviews every request and the final quote can change depending on the vehicle, access at pickup and drop-off, the route and timing. Your final quote will always be fair. Guaranteed.

How is the estimate calculated?

It starts from Jose's base in Escondido and adds up the drive to your pickup, the loaded miles to your drop-off and part of the drive home, diesel for the truck, driver time for driving and hookup, the tow fee for your type of vehicle, and any extras like a backyard pickup or a steep driveway.

Why does a backyard or tight spot change the price?

Backing an RV out of a backyard, through a tight gate or up a steep driveway takes more time and care. Some of these Jose wants to see in photos first, so the estimate flags them for review.

What happens after I send it?

Your details go to Jose by text or email, already filled in. He reviews them, asks for a photo if needed, and replies with your fair quote.

Tell Jose what needs to move

Call or text with the pickup, the drop-off and what you are hauling. You will get an honest answer and a fair quote, guaranteed.
